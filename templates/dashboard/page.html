Category Common Use Risk Score

Key Insights

  • May oxidize and cause allergic reactions
  • Linked to skin irritation in sensitive individuals

Risk Summary

Limonene is a common fragrance ingredient found in citrus oils. While generally safe, it can oxidize when exposed to air, which may cause allergic reactions or skin irritation in sensitive individuals.
