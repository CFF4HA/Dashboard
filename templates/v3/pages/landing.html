Our Methodology
Authoritative Ingredient Data

Ingredient records are sourced from PubChem, giving you toxicity info, hazard classifications, adverse effects, and regulatory data for each compound.

Community-Driven Tagging

You create custom tags that reflect your sensitivities, write pattern-based rules that automatically tag ingredients by their hazard labels, and share rule sets so others benefit from your research.

Product Intelligence

Tags and hazard data cascade from ingredients up to products. Filter and compare products by ingredient, tag, or hazard profile to find what to avoid and what's safe.
